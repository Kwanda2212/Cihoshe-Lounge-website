OUR STORY

Rooted in heritage.
Designed for modern gatherings.

Cihoshe Lounge is a place where African culinary heritage is honoured with elegance. Inspired by generations of shared tables, vibrant ingredients and evening rituals, we create dining experiences that feel intimate, celebratory and deeply memorable.

What we stand for

We celebrate bold African flavours, refined hospitality and thoughtful presentation — from comforting classics to contemporary signature dishes shaped by South African culture and community.

Our approach

Every dish at Cihoshe Lounge is built around freshness, authenticity and craftsmanship. We combine treasured ingredients with a modern culinary lens to create moments that feel luxurious yet welcoming.

WHY US

Warmth, flavour and connection.

From family dinners and special occasions to relaxed evenings with friends, our lounge brings people together through memorable food, music and atmosphere in the heart of Johannesburg.
